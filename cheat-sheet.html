Hyperboost

Hyperboost Edge

  • Foam: Hyperboost Pro (pelletized, from racing footwear)
  • Upper: PRIMEWEAVE + comfort pods
  • Outsole: LIGHTTRAXION
  • Stack/drop: 45mm / 39mm, 6mm
  • Feel: soft landing, springy plush toe-off
  • Pitch: "one pair that does it all, every day"
Adizero

Adios Pro 5

  • Midsole: 39mm LIGHTSTRIKE PRO + all-new carbon-infused ENERGYRIM
  • Upper: Engineered LIGHTLOCK 2.0 (more breathable) + heel foam pods
  • Outsole: LIGHTTRAXION + Continental™ forefoot
  • Stack/drop: 39mm / 34mm, 5mm
  • Weight: 6.3oz men's / 5.3oz women's
  • Feel: lightweight, propulsive, race-only
  • New vs. Pro 4: ENERGYRIMS replace EnergyRods 2.0, LIGHTLOCK 2.0, more foam underfoot, $250 → $275
  • Pitch: "our top-tier race-day shoe"
Adizero

Boston 13

  • Midsole/outsole: LIGHTSTRIKE PRO
  • Powered by EnergyRods (not a plate)
  • Feel: springy, versatile across paces
  • Pitch: "the one shoe for easy runs AND tempo"
Adizero

Evo SL Woven

  • Midsole: LIGHTSTRIKE PRO, plate-free (nylon dogbone stiffener)
  • Outsole: Continental + clear rubber
  • Stack/drop: 36mm / 29mm, 7mm
  • Feel: snappy, light, flexible — not plush
  • Pitch: "race-inspired speed for intervals/track, without Adios Pro's price"
Supernova

Supernova Rise 3

  • Midsole: Dreamstrike+ (softer/plusher than Lightstrike Pro)
  • Outsole: LIGHTTRAXION, full-length
  • Stack/drop: 37mm / 29mm, 8mm
  • Feel: most stable, most "grounded" in the lineup
  • Pitch: "on-feet all day? this is the one" — most budget-friendly too

Tech name cheat sheet

  • PRIMEWEAVE = woven upper (Hyperboost, Supernova)
  • LIGHTSTRIKE PRO = performance foam (Adios Pro, Boston, Evo SL)
  • HYPERBOOST PRO = comfort foam, race-derived (Hyperboost only)
  • DREAMSTRIKE+ = plush comfort foam (Supernova only)
  • EnergyRods = carbon rods shaped to metatarsals (Boston)
  • ENERGYRIM = new carbon-infused rim, replaces EnergyRods 2.0 (Adios Pro 5)
  • LIGHTLOCK 2.0 = breathable locking woven upper (Adios Pro 5)
  • Continental™ = adidas's outsole rubber partner, best wet-ground grip
  • LIGHTTRAXION = lightweight full-length outsole compound
