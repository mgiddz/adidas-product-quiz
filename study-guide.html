Footwear Pillars

Every running shoe lives in one of three pillars, from lightest & fastest to most supportive
  • Adizero — Light & Fast. The lightest, fastest shoes, built for racing and speed. In this program: Adios Pro 5, Evo SL Woven, Boston 13.
  • Hyperboost — Comfort Energized. Everyday comfort with energy. In this program: Hyperboost Edge.
  • Supernova — Supportive Comfort. The most supportive, most stable end of the line. In this program: Supernova Rise 3.
How to use it on the floor Find out whether the customer wants speed, energized everyday comfort, or support. That tells you which pillar to start in, and then which shoe.

Technology Glossary

The names you'll hear across every shoe in the lineup
PRIMEWEAVE

adidas's woven upper construction — super-soft, lightweight, and sock-like. Used across Hyperboost and Supernova for comfort-forward fit.

LIGHTSTRIKE PRO

adidas's premium performance foam — crafted in a non-compression molding process that reduces weight while boosting energy return. The shared DNA across the entire Adizero racing/training family (Adios Pro, Boston, Evo SL).

HYPERBOOST PRO

A pelletized foam derived from racing footwear, tuned specifically for the Hyperboost family's everyday-comfort positioning — energy return and cushioning without race-day firmness.

DREAMSTRIKE+

Supernova's cushioning foam — softer and more plush than Lightstrike Pro, built for all-day comfort rather than speed.

EnergyRods / EnergyRods 2.0

Carbon-infused rods embedded in the midsole, shaped to mimic the metatarsal bones. They add lightweight stiffness and limit energy loss, giving a more harmonious transfer of force through toe-off. Used in Boston (and the previous Adios Pro 4).

ENERGYRIM

New for the Adios Pro 5. A carbon-fiber-infused rim in the midsole that replaces EnergyRods 2.0, giving uninterrupted energy return directly underfoot, with higher energy return and more stability.

LIGHTLOCK 2.0

The Adios Pro 5's upper system: a one-way stretch woven with internal locking bands that contour the foot. Version 2.0 is engineered for better breathability than the original LIGHTLOCK.

Continental™ rubber

adidas's outsole rubber partnership with Continental tire — built for fast grip build-up on short ground-contact times, especially on wet surfaces.

LIGHTTRAXION

A lightweight full-length outsole compound with improved abrasion resistance, allowing thinner margins for grip without adding weight.

Stack height & drop

Stack height is the foam thickness under the heel and forefoot. Drop is the difference between the two (heel height minus forefoot height). A higher drop shifts impact toward the heel/hips/knees; a lower drop encourages a mid/forefoot strike and shifts more stress to the foot and ankle.

Why shoe weight matters

Weight carried on the feet costs roughly 5x the energy of the same weight carried on the torso — every step lifts and moves it. On a 10K (~13,000 steps), even a 100g difference adds up to over a kilogram of extra work over the run.
